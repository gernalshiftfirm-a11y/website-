Our story

A neighbourhood bakery, with big-city soul.

Born in Sangrur and made for everyone who believes a good day starts with warm bread and ends with rich cream. At Satija's, we mix tradition with modern cafe culture — handmade cakes, wood-fire pizzas, fresh pastas, and the kind of coffee you'd cross town for.

Whether it's a slice of truffle pastry on a quiet evening or a custom birthday cake for someone you love — we put care into every layer.
