Which classes are taught at SG Coaching Centre?

We offer Mathematics coaching for Class 9, 10, 11 and 12 students from CBSE & Punjab Board, along with crash courses, board exam preparation, and a competitive exam foundation program (JEE/NTSE/Olympiad).

Is there a demo class available?

Yes! We offer a 100% free demo class for every new student. You can book it directly from this website by filling the form in the “Free Demo” section — your details are sent to us instantly on WhatsApp.

Are online classes available?

Our flagship offering is offline classroom coaching at our Sangrur centre, but we provide online doubt sessions, recorded revisions and digital DPPs for all enrolled students.

What are the batch timings?

We run morning, afternoon and evening batches — typically 8:00 AM, 2:00 PM, 5:00 PM and 7:00 PM. Final timings are confirmed at admission based on your school schedule.

How does the test system work?

Students get daily practice sheets (DPPs), weekly chapter tests, monthly grand tests and full-length mocks before boards. Each test is reviewed in class with a performance report shared with parents.

What is the fees structure?

Fees vary by class, course duration and batch type. We keep our pricing transparent and student-friendly. Please contact us or book a demo for the latest fee details and seasonal offers.
