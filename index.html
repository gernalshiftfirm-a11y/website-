// What's Inside

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Step into a fully-loaded fitness floor designed for every goal — from your first pushup to your next personal record.

Strength Zone

Olympic platforms, power racks, deadlift bars, kettlebells & 5,000+ lbs of plates. Built for serious lifters.

Cardio Floor

40+ machines: treadmills, ellipticals, stair climbers, rowers, assault bikes & curved sprint tracks.

Functional Studio

Open-floor space for HIIT, kettlebell circuits, battle ropes, sleds & sandbags. Group classes daily.

CrossFit Box

Dedicated rig with rings, ropes, GHDs and pullup stations. Daily WODs led by L2-certified coaches.

Spin Studio

30 stadium-style spin bikes, immersive lighting & a sound system that turns every class into a concert.

Yoga & Mobility

Heated yoga room with bamboo flooring. Hatha, vinyasa, mobility & meditation classes 7 days a week.

Locker Rooms

Men's & women's premium locker rooms with rain showers, towel service, hair-dryers & toiletries.

Sauna & Steam

Finnish sauna and full-spectrum steam rooms — the perfect post-workout recovery ritual.

Smoothie Bar

Fresh post-workout smoothies, cold brew, protein shakes & healthy snacks. Members get 15% off.

Free Parking

Spacious well-lit parking — plus dedicated 2-wheeler bays. Park, train, leave. No hassle.

Personal Training

1-on-1 coaching tailored to your body, goals & schedule. Custom nutrition plans included.

Free Wi-Fi & App

Free high-speed Wi-Fi, plus our member app — book classes, track workouts, log progress.
