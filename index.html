// FAQs

Quick answers, straight up.

Do you provide personal training?

Yes — our PT package includes 12 dedicated sessions per month, a custom workout program, custom nutrition, and weekly check-ins with a senior coach. Trainer Anshul and Preet Ma'am also lead 1-on-1 specialised sessions.

What are the membership prices?

Plans start at ₹1,999/month and go up to ₹16,799/year — with discounts of up to 30% on longer commitments. Personal training packages start at ₹4,999/month. See the Plans section for full details.

Is a free trial session available?

Absolutely. Every new prospect gets one free trial session with a coach plus a complete tour of the facility. Book yours here — no card, no commitment.

What are your gym timings?

Monday to Saturday: 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Sunday: 6:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Annual members get 24×7 keycard access.

Is the gym beginner-friendly?

One of the most beginner-friendly gyms in Sangrur. Our coaches walk every new member through equipment basics, form, and a simple starter program. You'll never feel out of place.

Do you have transformation programs?

Yes — structured 12, 16 and 24-week transformation programs with weekly progress audits, periodised training, custom nutrition and supplementation guidance. See the Transformations section for member results.

Do you provide diet & nutrition consultation?

Coach Neha leads our nutrition programs — fully customised macros, meal templates, supplementation guidance and weekly check-ins. Available standalone or bundled with PT.

Where exactly are you located?

Singla Road, Near Dr Dharmpal Road, Mubarik Mehal Colony, Sangrur, Punjab 148001. Plenty of parking — see the map above.
