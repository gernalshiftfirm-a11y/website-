// FAQs

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before your first visit. Still curious? Talk to us.

Are dental procedures at your clinic painful?

Almost all our treatments — including root canals, extractions and implants — are performed under modern local anaesthesia and gentle techniques. The vast majority of patients report little to no discomfort during or after.

How much does a treatment cost?

Costs vary by procedure and material chosen (e.g., zirconia vs. ceramic crowns). After your consultation we provide a clear written estimate. We also offer no-cost EMI options for higher-value treatments.

Do you treat children?

Yes — our pediatric specialists handle everything from first dental visits and fluoride treatments to milk-tooth extractions and behaviour management for anxious children.

How long does a dental implant take?

The implant placement itself takes about 30–45 minutes per tooth. The full process — implant + crown — typically spans 3 to 6 months as the implant integrates with the bone. Same-day implant options are available in select cases.

Do you accept dental insurance?

We accept cashless treatment from several major insurers and provide all required documentation for reimbursement. Bring your insurance card or details to your consultation.

Are emergency appointments available?

Yes — we keep slots reserved daily for dental emergencies (severe pain, swelling, knocked-out teeth or trauma). Call +91 98151 71917 immediately and we'll see you the same day.
